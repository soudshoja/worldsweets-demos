Distribution FAQs

Does World Sweets supply supermarkets and hypermarkets?

Yes. Our Wholesale & Modern Trade channel supplies retailers, supermarkets, and hypermarkets across Kuwait. We provide promotional support, flyer campaigns, and in-house repacking services to maximize your shelf presence.

Can I order World Sweets products for catering and events?

Absolutely. Our Catering channel offers premium sweets and custom-made products tailored specifically for catering companies, corporate events, and their audiences. Contact us to discuss your custom arrangements.

Which delivery platforms does World Sweets use?

We deliver via the World Sweets app (our own platform) and partner with major delivery services including Talabat and Tawseel for convenient e-commerce ordering across Kuwait with fast, reliable delivery.

Where are the World Sweets showrooms located?

We operate two flagship showrooms: the Shuwaikh showroom in Kuwait City (550 m²) and the Fahaheel showroom in Southern Kuwait (650 m²). Both are open daily from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Visit us to browse our full range.
