Our story

Four decades of distributing joy across Kuwait.

Founded in 1986 as a family-run business, World Sweets has evolved into one of Kuwait's leading confectionery distributors. With decades of experience and a deep-rooted passion for sweets, we have built a strong portfolio of well-established brands and products. Today, we proudly serve all market segments across Kuwait, powered by a dedicated team of over 150 skilled professionals.
