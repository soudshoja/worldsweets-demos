How we reach the market

Five distribution channels, one trusted partner.

From walk-in showrooms to e-commerce, wholesale, and catering, World Sweets puts confectionery in every hand across Kuwait.

Showroom

Walk-in purchases through two showrooms in key areas, each with over 550 m² of showcase space.

E-Commerce

Direct delivery via the World Sweets app and partners such as Talabat and Tawseel.

Catering

Premium sweets and custom-made products tailored for catering companies and their audiences.

Wholesale & Modern Trade

Supplying supermarkets and hypermarkets, backed by promotions, flyers, and in-house repacking.

& Many More

Flexible channels and partnerships that adapt to the needs of a growing Kuwaiti market.
